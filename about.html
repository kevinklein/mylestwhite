About Myles

Myles T. White, founder of Myles T. White Construction LLC, brings over three decades of hands-on experience to residential remodeling in the Portland metro area. A third-generation carpenter, Myles began his career framing homes and built his expertise from the ground up. He specializes in kitchen and bathroom remodels, custom finish carpentry, and full home renovations, while also offering project management and consulting services. His background includes new home construction and additions, giving him a well-rounded understanding of every phase of a project.

Myles is known for his detail-oriented craftsmanship and his ability to coordinate projects seamlessly alongside a trusted network of skilled subcontractors. His approach is rooted in integrity, clear communication, sustainability, and maintaining safe, clean job sites.

A lifelong member of the Portland community, Myles lives in Lake Oswego with his wife Elisa, who manages office operations and project coordination. Together, they've raised two adult children and enjoy spending time outdoors, attending music and sports events, and being with family and friends.

For Myles, the most important part of any project is the final result—delivering a space that homeowners love and can take pride in for years to come.
